# Vaporwave VHS Dream

![Vaporwave VHS Dream](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/vaporwave/cover-10ed6bda.avif)

A vaporwave film: sliced sunset, neon grid floor, palm trees, marble busts and VHS tape.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) set in a vaporwave VHS dream, with a sliced pink sunset, a neon perspective grid floor, palm trees, marble busts and the artifacts of home-recorded tape. Use it for internet, music and 1990s nostalgia topics, or to give an explanation a dreamy retro mood.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.

Kits: [Retro computers and screens](../../kits/retro-screens/README.md)

Preview and details: https://gsartori.com/skills/vaporwave
