# GTA 6

Video styles and interactive components inspired by the GTA 6 opening, with layered silhouettes, engravings, enamel and coastal light.

Previews and the copy button: https://gsartori.com/design-systems/palma-deco

## How to use

Paste [rules.md](rules.md) into any AI and ask for what you need: a landing page, a component or a video. The rules set the colors, fonts, borders and motion; your AI adapts any piece to them. Start from a component below when you want working code.
