# Ukiyo-e Woodblock Scroll

![Ukiyo-e Woodblock Scroll](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/ukiyo-e-woodblock/cover-ae32d442.avif)

A film styled as a Japanese woodblock scroll: unrolling paper, blue waves, golden clouds and red seals.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) styled as a Japanese woodblock print unrolling right to left, with mulberry paper, carved outlines, blue waves, golden cloud dividers and red seal stamps. Use it to tell something in chapters with a calm, classic feel, such as the stages of a project or the members of a team.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.

Kits: [Print and newsreel](../../kits/print-and-newsreel/README.md), [Traditional crafts](../../kits/traditional-crafts/README.md)

Preview and details: https://gsartori.com/skills/ukiyo-e-woodblock
