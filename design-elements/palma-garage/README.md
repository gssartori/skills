# Garage shutter

![Garage shutter](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/palma-garage/cover-e25a2860.avif)

Raise a slatted garage door to reveal a coral coupe inside.

Raise a slatted garage door to reveal a coral coupe and its name, and lower it again to hide them. Use it to show and hide content with a touch of suspense, such as a product, a new release or an extra detail. An interactive piece from the GTA 6 design system with keyboard and reduced-motion support.
