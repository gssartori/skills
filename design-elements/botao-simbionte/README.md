# Symbiote Button

![Symbiote Button](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/botao-simbionte/cover-26632928.avif)

A button of living black matter: elastic strands grow from its edges, grab the cursor, stretch and snap.

A pill button of soft matte black matter with a cool silver sheen. When the cursor comes close, elastic strands grow from the edges, stretch toward it, cross into webs, thin out and snap, and each fragment retracts along its own path. Holding still near the edge strengthens the bond and its reach; shaking weakens it. It is the special piece of the Symbiote design system, for the signature moment of a landing page, an interactive video or a brand. It stays a real button, keyboard included, and respects reduced motion. Inspired by the Venom Button by exteestudio (github.com/exteestudio/venom-button); the code was written from scratch.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, say where the control goes (landing page, app, video) and what it does, and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). It delivers the code in HTML and JavaScript, with WebGL and a fallback without GPU.
