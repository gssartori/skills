# Lighthouse annotations

![Lighthouse annotations](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/palma-beacon/cover-fe480ace.avif)

Select a landmark on the illustrated coast to reveal its anchored annotation.

Select a landmark on the illustrated coast to reveal its annotation, linked to the spot by a short leader line. Use it to explain the parts of an image, a map or a product without taking people away from the picture. An interactive piece from the GTA 6 design system with keyboard and reduced-motion support.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt and describe where the component will appear. For motion graphics, specify when the gesture and state change happen.
