# Textbook Cutaway

![Textbook Cutaway](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/textbook-cutaway/cover-1cf564ed.avif)

A science-textbook film: a cutaway block with layers, ducts and precise labels.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) as a science textbook plate, where the subject becomes a block cut in half, assembled layer by layer, with a warm flow traveling through ducts and each part named by a callout line. Use it to explain how something works inside, such as a system, a machine or the path of a message, a product or energy.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.

Kits: [Hand-drawn](../../kits/hand-drawn/README.md)
