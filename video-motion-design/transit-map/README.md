# Metro Transit Diagram

![Metro Transit Diagram](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/transit-map/cover-49ad6607.avif)

A film styled as a metro map: coloured lines at 45 degrees, stations, capsule trains and an amber LED board.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) styled as a schematic subway map on off-white paper, with coloured lines at 45 and 90 degrees, interchange stations, capsule trains and an amber LED departure board. Use it to show how several parts connect, such as teams, systems or routes meeting at a central hub.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.
