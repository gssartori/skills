# Palma Deco

![Palma Deco](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/palma-deco/cover-faadfcd9.avif)

Art Deco silhouettes reveal a layered scene in two or three colors. A thin neon edge travels around the contour and leads into the next shot.

Turn a brief into a coastal title sequence with patterned apertures, partially lit edges, paired typography and a synthesized score.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content section with your story and ask your AI for a self-contained HTML film with sound. Export to MP4 in an environment with video rendering.

Tags: Video.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.
