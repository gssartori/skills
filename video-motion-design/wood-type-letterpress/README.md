# Wood-Type Letterpress

![Wood-Type Letterpress](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/wood-type-letterpress/cover-81d20578.avif)

A wood-type poster film: black and red ink stamped onto thick textured paper.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) as a hand-printed wood-type poster: large letters, carved illustrations and ornaments in black and red. Wood grain, squeezed ink, worn edges, slight misregistration and paper impressions reveal the printing process.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.
