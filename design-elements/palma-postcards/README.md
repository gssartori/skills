# Coastal postcards

![Coastal postcards](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/palma-postcards/cover-92fd99ad.avif)

Leaf through three illustrated postcards as each destination swings to the front of the stack.

Leaf through three illustrated postcards in either direction as each destination swings to the front of the stack. Use it to present a few options one at a time, such as destinations, products or testimonials. An interactive piece from the GTA 6 design system with keyboard and reduced-motion support.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt and describe where the component will appear. For motion graphics, specify when the gesture and state change happen.
