# City light

## Look

Cut a narrow, rounded window through a copper frame. Draw a low coastal skyline with stepped towers and a near palm. Warm peach sky describes the day. Night uses plum air, stars and small amber windows.

## Interaction

Use a switch with a stable name. Move the sun medallion from the left stop to the right as the sky changes exposure and the windows light up. Exchange its engraved sun for a crescent. Reverse cleanly on a second tap. Position must communicate the result without relying on color.

## Delivery

Deliver a standalone offline HTML component with inline CSS, original vector art and local JavaScript. Use Brazilian Portuguese labels for this deployment. Keep the control bounded inside its gallery frame. Provide a visible keyboard focus state, touch operation and an immediate transition for reduced motion. Hover adds feedback without carrying any essential action. Use no network requests or browser storage.
