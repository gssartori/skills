# Aero Glass Desktop

![Aero Glass Desktop](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/aero-glass-desktop/cover-f6ec35e4.avif)

A film inside a late-2000s glass desktop: translucent windows, aurora light and a taskbar.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) inside a late-2000s frosted-glass operating system, where each part of your subject becomes a translucent window, with aurora light bands and soft electric piano. Use it to explain a technology topic, an app or a step-by-step process with the nostalgia of computers from that era.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.

Kits: [Retro computers and screens](../../kits/retro-screens/README.md)

Preview and details: https://gsartori.com/skills/aero-glass-desktop
