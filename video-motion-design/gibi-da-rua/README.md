# Gibi da Rua

![Gibi da Rua](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/gibi-da-rua/cover-68217469.avif)

A Brazilian neighborhood comic film: a group of children, a tin-can band and a capybara by the bakery.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) inspired by newsstand comic covers: a sunny Brazilian neighborhood street, a gang of children telling your story and a band playing instruments made from cans. Use it for cheerful, familiar stories with a Brazilian feel that should appeal to all ages.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.

Kits: [Manga and comics](../../kits/manga-and-comics/README.md)

Preview and details: https://gsartori.com/skills/gibi-da-rua
