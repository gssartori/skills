# Radar destination

![Radar destination](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/palma-route/cover-17d946b1.avif)

Choose a destination to trace a route along the canal streets.

Choose a destination to trace the route to it along the canal streets, one destination at a time. Use it to show routes and places, such as the way to a store, an event or the stops of a tour. An interactive piece from the GTA 6 design system with keyboard and reduced-motion support.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt and describe where the component will appear. For motion graphics, specify when the gesture and state change happen.
