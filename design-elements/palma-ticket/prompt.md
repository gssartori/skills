# Boarding coupon

Validate a boarding pass to tear away its stub and expose a registered seal.

## Art direction and motion

Print a skewed cream ferry pass with small boarding details, irregular-width barcode and perforated stub. Pull the stub out and tip it around its lower hinge to expose a cancellation seal. Keep the detached stub clickable to restore the pass. Keep the layout anchored throughout.

## Interaction and video

Keep the interactive version operable with a pointer, touch and the keyboard. Retain the chosen state once the motion settles. For a film, stage the same input as a deliberate gesture, hold the initial and final states long enough to read, and drive the transition from the composition clock. Keep key values visible when reduced motion is requested.

Deliver a self-contained HTML component that works offline in a sandboxed catalog card. Use original inline vector artwork, CSS and local JavaScript. Keep Brazilian Portuguese interface labels, high-contrast focus rings and legible figures at small sizes. Use no external assets or browser storage.
