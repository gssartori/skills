# Shine Button

![Shine Button](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/botao-brilho/cover-7af5d539.avif)

A pill button with a passing shine that lifts on hover and confirms the click with a check.

A primary action button shaped like a pill, with a gradient, a light streak that crosses it every few seconds and speeds up on hover, a subtle lift under the pointer, a ripple on click and a switch to "Copied" with a check that draws itself. Use it as the buy or sign-up button of a landing page, the Copy button of a code block, the closing call to action of a video or the primary button of your design system. It works in both light and night themes and respects reduced motion.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, say where the button goes (landing page, app, video) and what it does, and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for your colors, label and click action; it delivers the code in HTML and CSS, React or whatever your project uses.
