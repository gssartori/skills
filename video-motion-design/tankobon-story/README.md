# Tankobon Story

![Tankobon Story](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/tankobon-story/cover-4471fe16.avif)

A printed black-and-white manga film: halftone screens, tilted panels and hand-drawn sound effects.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with drawing-desk sounds) that resembles printed manga, with ink and paper, dotted screens, tilted panels and katakana sound effects drawn into the scene. Use it to tell a story with the drama and pace of manga, with emotion and sound at every turn.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.

Kits: [Manga and comics](../../kits/manga-and-comics/README.md)

Preview and details: https://gsartori.com/skills/tankobon-story
