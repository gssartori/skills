# Topographic Survey

![Topographic Survey](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/topographic-survey/cover-0821ac00.avif)

A film styled as an antique topographic survey: contour lines on tinted paper, a dashed crimson trail and a valley lake.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) styled as an antique hand-tinted survey map, with contour lines, shaded relief, ink waterways, a compass rose and a dashed crimson trail descending to a valley lake. Use it to show a path, a step-by-step process or the terrain of a problem, with the feel of an explorer's map.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.
