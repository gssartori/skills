# Aero Glass Desktop

![Aero Glass Desktop](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/aero-glass-desktop/cover-f6ec35e4.avif)

A film inside a late-2000s glass desktop: translucent windows, aurora light and a taskbar.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) inside a frosted-glass operating system: diagonal window reflections, aurora light bands, perspective window stacks and a glowing start button. Soft electric piano underpins the scenes. Each part of your subject becomes a window.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.
