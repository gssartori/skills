# Tankobon Story

![Tankobon Story](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/tankobon-story/cover-4471fe16.avif)

A printed black-and-white manga film: halftone screens, tilted panels and hand-drawn sound effects.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with drawing-desk sounds) that resembles printed manga: ink and paper, dotted screens, tilted panels and katakana sound effects drawn into the scene. Describe your subject and the prompt composes the page.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Tags: Video.
