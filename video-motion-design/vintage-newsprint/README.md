# Vintage Newsprint

![Vintage Newsprint](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/vintage-newsprint/cover-94c2b2f3.avif)

A 1950s newspaper film: yellowed paper, halftone illustrations and red-pencil annotations.

Create an animated JavaScript film (1920×1080, with sound) on the front page of a 1950s newspaper, with aged paper, halftone illustrations, ruled columns and red-pencil notes, where each part of the subject becomes an article. Use it to announce news or tell a fact as a headline, with the look of an old newspaper.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, replace the Content brief with your subject and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). Ask for an HTML film with sound; MP4 export depends on the rendering tools available in your workflow.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.

Kits: [Print and newsreel](../../kits/print-and-newsreel/README.md)

Preview and details: https://gsartori.com/skills/vintage-newsprint
