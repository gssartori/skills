# Symbiote Button

![Symbiote Button](https://media.gsartori.com/skills/simbionte-botao/cover-26632928.avif)

A button of living black matter: elastic strands grow from its edges, grab the cursor, stretch and snap.

A pill button of soft matte black matter where elastic strands grow from the edges, stretch toward the cursor, cross into webs and snap, with a stronger bond when the cursor holds still nearby. Use it for the signature moment of a landing page, an interactive video or a brand, as the special piece of the Symbiote design system. It stays a real button, keyboard included, respects reduced motion and was inspired by the Venom Button by exteestudio (github.com/exteestudio/venom-button), with the code written from scratch.

## How to use

Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and give its instructions to your AI.

Copy the prompt, say where the control goes (landing page, app, video) and what it does, and give it to your AI (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Kimi). It delivers the code in HTML and JavaScript, with WebGL and a fallback without GPU.

Works with: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Kimi.
